## Make your CRM easier to use before adding more software

When leads, follow-ups, projects and reporting live in different tools, it is hard to see what needs attention. Clydara can help map the work your team actually does, identify the data and handoffs that matter, and turn that into a practical implementation scope.

The Blueprint is for a service business considering a CRM setup, dashboard, client portal, or workflow automation. It starts with the problem and existing tools. A custom build is one possible outcome; configuring what you already use may be the better choice.

**CTA:** [Tell us about your current workflow](/contact)

## What the blueprint would cover

1. **Current workflow:** How a lead arrives, becomes a client, moves through delivery, and gets followed up.
2. **Data and access:** The records, fields, permissions and integrations needed for a reliable view of work.
3. **Priority screens and automations:** Which dashboard decisions matter and where a trigger could remove repetitive work.
4. **Build-vs-configure recommendation:** What can be solved in an existing platform, what needs custom development, and why.
5. **Phased scope:** A first release, later enhancements, dependencies, risks and acceptance criteria to support an informed quote.

The precise deliverables, fee and schedule would be agreed in writing after reviewing the current systems. Implementation, licenses, migrations and third-party subscriptions are separate unless explicitly included in that agreement.

## Who this fits

- Service teams whose follow-ups or project handoffs are hard to track.
- Businesses with a CRM that staff avoid because it requires too much manual updating.
- Founders deciding whether to build a client portal or use existing software.

It is less useful when the buyer already has a complete technical specification and only needs a clearly scoped implementation quote.

## Questions we would ask first

- What falls through the cracks today: new leads, next actions, client updates, reporting, or something else?
- Which systems hold the source data, and who can grant appropriate access?
- What should each role be able to see or change?
- What decision should a dashboard help someone make each day?
- What must work in the first release, and what can wait?
