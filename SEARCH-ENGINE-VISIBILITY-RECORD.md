## Meaning of the records

- A search result means the specific page appeared in that query/session. It does not establish every page is indexed or a commercial ranking position.
- An answer citation means the product linked the first-party domain in its completed answer. An unlinked brand mention, a directory citation and prompted URL retrieval are separate outcomes.
- No results means the tested supported query returned no matches. Qwant/Yahoo/Mojeek are not relabelled as globally deindexed based on one query. Mojeek's unsupported bare site: syntax is excluded.
- A CAPTCHA, access block or unexecuted pair is unmeasured, not a zero result.
- Raw visitor data, sign-in URLs/tokens, account history, birth-year data and secrets are excluded. Grok's response was verified in product text; its empty screenshot was excluded.
- The snapshots predate some source refreshes. AI answers repeating the old email are historical observations; the owner-confirmed address is now clydara1@gmail.com.
